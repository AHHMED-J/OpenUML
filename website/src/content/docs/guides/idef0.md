IDEF0 describes a process as boxes (functions) with four kinds of arrows: inputs from the left, controls from the top, outputs to the right and mechanisms from below.

In OpenUML you can number boxes automatically, decompose a box into its own child diagram, connect the parent's arrows to the boxes that use them, and check the diagram with the consistency warnings.

The full guide is a PDF for now: [IDEF0 guide (English)](https://github.com/AHHMED-J/OpenUML/raw/main/docs/guides/OpenUML_IDEF0_Guide_EN.pdf).
